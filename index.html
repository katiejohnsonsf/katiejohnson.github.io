What I'm Working On

I'm a developer at Informed Seattle — a proof of concept for information infrastructure that translates Seattle City Council legislation into accessible weekly summaries. The application crawls Seattle's Legistar legislative database, runs AI-generated summaries through a chunked LLM pipeline, and deploys structured plain-language bill summaries via GitHub Pages. I'm a former Emerging Technology Fellow at the U.S. Census Bureau's xD cohort, where I collaborated with international partners on secure multiparty computation and AI governance frameworks for the federal data science lifecycle.

My previous work includes ML engineering at Mirror Labs (NLP-to-music generation pipelines), data analysis at Recurve (energy efficiency data products for the California PUC), and UX design at Accela (gov tech for permitting and licensing). I hold a Certificate in Data Science from Galvanize and a B.A. in Environmental Science from Mills College.
