I help social impact organizations build data pipelines and tools to hold themselves accountable—as a data scientist, researcher, and Citizen.

I am curious about how programs, services, and policies can be designed to actually change the lives of the people they're trying to serve. I see my work catalyzing impact for vulnerable populations, human and beyond, by first collaboratively framing problems and solutions.

I define impact at the intersection of both technical and social systems to create impact. I test assumptions through coded proofs of concept that I can evaluate according to the stakeholder theory of change. I care about who gets access, whose voice is considered, and what infrastructure makes participation possible.
